# Vocabulary

Controlled terminology. Figures marked 2026 change every January — date-stamp anything you copy out of here.

---

## Soccer, not football

**Absolute in North American context.** The league is Major League Soccer; the site is mlssoccer.com. "Football" is permitted only as a second-reference variant when discussing the global game.

**Québec French also uses *soccer*.** CF Montréal's own French copy reads *"club canadien de soccer."* This is a real distinction from European French, which uses *football*. **Do not "correct" it.**

## Preferred terms

| Preferred | Discouraged |
|---|---|
| match | game *(permitted, less preferred)* |
| pitch | field |
| kit | jersey |
| head coach | manager |
| goalkeeper | goalie |
| roster | squad *(permitted)* |
| offside | offsides, off-side |
| stoppage time / added time | injury time |
| club, team, side | **franchise (prohibited)** |
| Matchday | gameday |

Two honest caveats: the *match/pitch/kit* preferences come from Atlanta United's published guide rather than from MLS itself, though observed league usage matches them. And the league's own roster documents say **"gameday roster"**, not "matchday roster" — so Matchday over gameday is a club preference, not league-uniform.

**"Franchise" is the strongest prohibition** in the ecosystem. It reads as American-major-league and cuts directly against the global-football positioning.

Exact styling. These are the errors that most obviously mark copy as not-from-MLS.

| Correct | Notes |
|---|---|
| **MLS Cup** | Year goes **after**: *MLS Cup 2025*. Sponsored: *MLS Cup 2025 presented by Audi*. Trophy: Philip F. Anschutz Trophy. |
| **Audi 2026 MLS Cup Playoffs** | **Sponsor first, then year** — the opposite construction to MLS Cup. Second reference: "MLS Cup Playoffs". |

## Roster mechanics

All proper nouns. 2026 figures.

**Salary Budget** — $6,425,000 per club, covering the 20-player Senior Roster.
**Maximum Salary Budget Charge** — $803,125. The threshold that defines a DP.

**Designated Player (DP)** — allows up to three players whose total compensation and acquisition costs exceed the Maximum Salary Budget Charge. The charge against the budget is capped regardless of actual salary; the club or owner covers the excess.

> **The error to avoid:** a DP is *not* "a player paid over the cap." It is a player whose **budget charge** is capped while their **actual compensation** exceeds the maximum. The rule is a charge mechanism, not a spending exemption.

2026 charges: age 24+ `$803,125`; 21–23 `$200,000`; 20 and under `$150,000`. The lower two tiers are **Young Designated Player** categories.

**General Allocation Money (GAM)** — $3,280,000 per club. **Tradable.** Does not expire. Used to buy down budget charges, including DP charges.

**Targeted Allocation Money (TAM)** — $2,125,000 per club. **Not tradable.** Used to sign or re-sign players earning above the Maximum Salary Budget Charge.

> One-line contrast, and the distinction most often got wrong: **GAM is tradable and general-purpose; TAM is non-tradable and targeted at players above the max charge.**

**Homegrown Player** — a player signed without entering the SuperDraft, having spent at least one year in that club's academy. No limit per year.

**SuperDraft** — one word, internal capital D. Official first reference is the sponsored form.

**Discovery** — up to five players on a club's Discovery List at a time, no limit on signings from it. Priority goes to the earliest filing; priority can be bought for $50,000 in GAM. MLS's own terms are *Discovery List*, *Discovery Process*, *Discovery Priority*. **"Discovery Claim" is colloquial, not league vocabulary.**

**U22 Initiative** — the league's current styling (Atlanta's guide says "Under-22 Initiative"; prefer the league form). Three or four slots, each occupying a Senior Roster slot. Player must be 22 or younger in their first eligible year and may hold the slot through the year they turn 25. Choosing four slots adds $2 million in use-it-this-season GAM.

**Also:** Senior Roster (1–20) · Supplemental Roster (21–31) · Off-Budget · International Roster Slots (241 total across 30 clubs in 2026, tradable) · Reserve Minimum Salary · Generation adidas (lowercase adidas) · Primary and Secondary Transfer Windows.

**Organisations:** Professional Referee Organization (PRO) — parenthesise on first reference · MLS Players Association → the Players Association → MLSPA (third reference only) · Black Players for Change (BPC).

## Match terminology

| Term | Styling |
|---|---|
| **matchday** | Lowercase generically; capitalised when numbered (*Matchday 19*) or as a product |
| **starting XI** | Roman numerals, capital XI. Not "first XI" |
| **hat trick** | **Two words, lowercase.** Never hat-trick or hattrick |
| **brace** | Two goals. Unitalicised, unglossed |
| **clean sheet** | Interchangeable with *shutout* |
| **golazo** | Unitalicised, unglossed, fine in headlines |
| **set piece** | Free kicks, corners, throw-ins. **Never goal kicks** |
| **penalty kick** | Full term first reference; *penalty* after |
| **minutes** | Ordinal + word: *"a 91st-minute winner," "in the 96th minute."* The `45+2'` format does not appear in MLS prose |

**Derbies.** MLS uses "derby" and "rivalry" interchangeably and does not enforce official names. Verified names it does use: **El Tráfico** (accent on the á) · **Hell is Real** · **Texas Derby** · **Rocky Mountain Cup** · **Cascadia Cup** · **Canadian Classique** · **Florida Derby**. Notably MLS leaves several rivalries unnamed in its own coverage ("the battle for New York").

Many derby names are supporter-originated, and the league appears to use only those with organic traction rather than imposing names. **Never fabricate a rivalry name.**

## Club names

The full table with tricodes, colours and confidence ratings is [13 Clubs](13-clubs.md) and `data/clubs.json`.

**There is no rule for suffix placement. It is a lookup table.**

- **FC leads:** FC Cincinnati, FC Dallas
- **FC trails:** Atlanta United FC, Austin FC, Charlotte FC, Chicago Fire FC, Houston Dynamo FC, Minnesota United FC, New York City FC, San Diego FC, Seattle Sounders FC, Toronto FC, Vancouver Whitecaps FC
- **CF leads:** CF Montréal · **CF trails:** Inter Miami CF
- **SC trails:** Nashville SC, Orlando City SC, St. Louis CITY SC
- **No suffix:** Colorado Rapids, Columbus Crew, D.C. United, LA Galaxy, New England Revolution, New York Red Bulls, Philadelphia Union, Portland Timbers, Real Salt Lake, San Jose Earthquakes, Sporting Kansas City

**The tricky ones:**

- **CF Montréal** — the é is mandatory in all published copy.
- **St. Louis CITY SC** — CITY all caps, per the club's own site. MLS's scores page renders "City SC", contradicting the club. **Follow the club.**
- **New York Red Bulls** vs **Red Bull New York** — not interchangeable. *Red Bull New York* is the corporate entity name. *New York Red Bulls* is the team name used in headlines, match content and standings. For editorial, use New York Red Bulls; RBNY after.
- **LA Galaxy** — no periods. **D.C. United** — periods. Both, in the same sentence, correctly.
- **LAFC** — never "LA FC" with a space. MLS's directory uses the full "Los Angeles Football Club"; LAFC is safe nearly everywhere.
- **Sporting Kansas City** — never "Kansas City Sporting". *Sporting KC* is second reference only.

**Club names take plural verbs.** "Austin FC **are** playing." "Inter Miami **have** climbed the mountaintop." This deviates from AP style and is the most-violated MLS convention.
