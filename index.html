Thirty clubs

The bar is each club's true primary, secondary and tertiary, never altered. The button is the contrast-corrected UI accent. Where a primary is achromatic (LAFC, D.C. United) the accent is promoted to a chromatic club colour instead of being lightened into grey.

Documents & decks

Word and PowerPoint are first-class outputs, generated from these same tokens rather than exported from the web layer. A slide is read from the back of a room and a document at arm's length, so each gets its own point-based scale.
