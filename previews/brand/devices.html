Crest slot — no marks ship in this repo

Enforces the published 4×-X clear space by computing padding from its own size, plus the 32px minimum. A crest shows the mark for its own data-tricode once one is installed, and the lettered socket until then.

The socket, forced with data-placeholder. Deliberately ugly: a placeholder that looks finished is a placeholder that ships. It stays a socket even when the mark exists, because “no asset here” is a statement an author gets to make.
