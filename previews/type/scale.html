Matchday 24 · Providence Park

Portland leave Seattle with three points

Portland Timbers are back in the playoff places after a 2–1 win at Lumen Field, their first away victory in the Cascadia derby since 2019. Luiz Araújo opened the scoring with a golazo from the edge of the area, and a stoppage-time header sealed it.

Note three things in that sentence: the plural verb on the club name, the unitalicised and unglossed golazo, and the preserved diacritic in Araújo. All three are house style, and all three are the kind of detail that marks copy as genuinely MLS.
