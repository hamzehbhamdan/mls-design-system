Slide designs

The deck layer in templates/js/mls-deck.cjs carries thirty layouts in three designs. Not three colour schemes — three ideas about what a slide is. Each design resolves three surfaces and maps every layout to one by role: stage for openers and statements, page for content, data for tables. A layout never picks its own colours, which is why one set of functions serves all three. press is the default: poster-scale type with the Record Book's restraint, and no dependency on imagery.

press — navy type pages alternate with white paper pages. The default.
